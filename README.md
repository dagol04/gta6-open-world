**Solmar** — an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. You are Danny "Diz" Marlowe, a wheelman fresh out of prison, working jobs for the fixer Mara Voss across the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar PD as your wanted level climbs, take on a six-mission story, fight rival crews, and build your reputation — all in a desktop browser from static files, with no network request.

Everything is original: the city of Solmar, its districts, characters, vehicles, story, art, sounds and music. Nothing from Rockstar Games or any other commercial game.

Features: a drivable open city with traffic and pedestrians, a 0–5 star wanted system with police cars, foot cops, roadblocks and a helicopter, six story missions with waypoints and rewards, combat on foot and from cars, shops (guns, clinic, spray shop), a live minimap HUD, a day/night cycle with headlights and neon, synthesized engine/siren/gunshot audio and a procedural radio soundtrack, and a save that persists in your browser.

## Run it

```sh
npx serve game
# then open http://localhost:3000 in desktop Chrome
# or: python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Controls: WASD / arrows to move or drive, E to enter or exit a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, R to reload, F to punch, Shift to sprint, Space for handbrake, M to mute, P or Esc to pause. Your progress saves automatically.
